GDPR & Privacy Policy

KRO Welfare Group

1. Introduction

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679, KRO Welfare Group has a legal duty to protect any information collected from members and users.

2. Information We Collect
  • Name and contact details
  • Membership information
  • Payment records
  • Uploaded supporting documents
  • Welfare-related information
3. Purpose of Data Use

Your information is used strictly for purposes related to the objectives and operations of the KRO Welfare Group, including:

  • Membership management
  • Welfare administration
  • Payment processing
  • Communication and notifications
  • Claims and document handling
4. Data Protection

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

5. Data Sharing

Your information will not be sold or shared outside the organization except where legally required or necessary for services directly related to welfare operations.

6. Your Rights
  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
Declaration

By registering with the KRO Welfare Group, you agree that the information provided may be used solely for the purposes stated in the organization objectives and operations.
